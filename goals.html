Goal 1 — Strategic Initiative
AI-Supported Design of Teaching and Learning Resources

Connected to ISNS's Digital and Media Literacy (AI) strategic initiative, this goal explores how AI can support the design of clearer, better-scaffolded teaching resources — worksheets, worked solutions, and presentation materials — while keeping sound pedagogy and student learning at the centre of the process. Resources will be trialled with students and refined throughout the year based on their work, questions, and feedback.
